# Announcement banner art

One image per announcement, shown in the banner on the player Home screen (phone and desktop).
Staff upload it in HQ under Banners. `hololive-banner.jpg` in this folder is a sample.

## File format
- **1600 x 1000 px (8:5)**, JPG or WebP (PNG and GIF also work), **under 5 MB** (the upload limit). Aim for about 400 KB.
- Full-bleed art that fills the whole image. Do not leave empty or flat-color areas at the edges.
- **No text baked into the image.** The app adds the title, subtitle, badge and buttons on top.

## Where things go (the image is cropped to different shapes)
- On a phone the banner is about 360 x 170 px (wider than the image), so the **top and bottom get cropped**.
- On desktop it is about 500 px wide and 300 to 400 px tall, so the **sides, top and bottom can all be trimmed** a little.
- **Subject safe zone:** keep the main subject (a face, a character, a product) inside the **middle 60% of the width** and **between 150 and 800 px from the top**.
- **Bottom third (about 330 px):** keep it calm, dark to mid tones, low detail. The title and subtitle sit bottom-left and the Twitch and YouTube buttons sit bottom-right, over a dark gradient.
- **Top-left corner:** keep it clear. The badge ("Fun", "LIVE") goes there.
- **Top-right corner:** keep it clear. The slide dots go there.
- Bold, simple shapes read best at phone size. Avoid fine detail and small writing.

## Check before sending
1. Shrink the image to 360 px wide: is the subject still clear?
2. Cover the bottom third and the two top corners: is anything important hidden?
3. Does the art reach all four edges with no blank band?
