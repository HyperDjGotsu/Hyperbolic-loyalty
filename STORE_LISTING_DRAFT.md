## Full description
Player Pass connects you to your local game store.

- See upcoming events, tap Interested, and share them with friends
- Check in at the store with a QR code or NFC card
- Earn XP and points for playing, climb the store leaderboard, level up
- Spin the daily wheel and vote on the Card of the Day
- Spend points in the Prize Wall
- Add friends, see who is playing, and report or block anyone
- Get reminders for events you care about

Passes (Bronze to Diamond) are sold in the store, not in the app.
